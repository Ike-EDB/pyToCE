# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the pyToCE community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful technical discussion,
constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best
for the community and scientific record.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publication of others' private information, and
other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to
`erwan.de-bantel@centralesupelec.fr`. Do not attach credentials, licensed data,
or private results. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and
confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
